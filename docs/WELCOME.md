# Welcome to TheDojo

For the AI reading this: speak this to the human almost as written. Do not turn it into a resume or a company explainer. After you finish, offer doors and wait: start from scratch (`docs/LAUNCH.md`), inspect an existing setup (`inspect-os` / Door B), or look around. Do not run the identity interview until they ask.

Welcome to TheDojo. Where knowledge is shared.

TheDojo was created by me, Michael Billett Jr., after experiencing the challenges of integrating AI inside an established organization. I kept meeting the same person: curious about AI, drowning in tools, and still not sure where to actually start. I was that person too. I just refused to stay there.

I come from sales. Not the slide-deck kind. The kind where you learn about the people, employees and customers, by standing next to them while the work is happening. While at Tesla, that turned into systems: store productivity, web growth, sales ops, and national training ops. I got obsessed with a simple question: how do you take what works in one room and make it work everywhere else, without forcing the humans in those rooms to become someone else?

At Opendoor I continued to lead sales while exploring how I could support both the employee and the customer journey, from acquisition to resale. The one constant was the chance to improve the productivity of every human through empowerment and enablement. So I built my first dojo, Superdojo, which brought production AI to sales, resale, product, growth, AI engineers, software engineers, new hires, and executives across the company. I am no software engineer. I am an operator who was given the chance to learn, and I learned to ship today's solutions. Contracts, memory, tests, human gates. Draft is not send. If it cannot be proven, it does not get a victory lap.

My AI journey started with ChatGPT, Glean, and LibreChat: asking questions and building repeatable templates for reports.
Then Claude Desktop and Gumloop for workflow automation.
After a brief chat with an AI engineer, he strongly encouraged me to use Claude Code in the terminal.
Now I use Cursor with multi-agent orchestration to build, test, and evaluate skills, scripts, and workflows stored in a repo.

A new or existing employee launching AI can clone Superdojo and step into company knowledge, execution speed, and room to tinker: tools, custom extensions, and apps.

That system is private to my organization. TheDojo is the public practice: start from scratch, or bring the setup you already have.

Mike
