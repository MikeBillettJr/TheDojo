# Identity interview (core deck)

Ask **3 questions per turn**. Stop after **9 asked** unless the operator says continue.

After each turn: **draft** updates to `memory/PROFILE.md`, `memory/agent-interface.md`, and `memory/non-negotiables.md`. Show summary; wait for approval before write.

Before asking, skip items already answered in PROFILE (name what you skip). On CONFLICT, surface both versions — do not overwrite silently.

## Round 1 — non-negotiables

1. What must never get traded away (family, health, money floor, time) even for a great opportunity?
2. When two good options conflict, how do you actually decide (gut, numbers, who you ask)?
3. What does a week look like when life is working — and what is the first sign it is not?

## Round 2 — taste and identity

4. What makes a person, company, or idea a **wow** for you (concrete tells, not adjectives)?
5. How do you want to be described when you are not in the room — and which labels are wrong for you?
6. What will you **not** become in the next five years, even if it paid more?

## Round 3 — how the agent should work with you

7. How should agents talk to you (pace, bluntness, teaching) when you are tired vs when you are building?
8. Which facts should live only in a local private note — never in Git memory or shared cards?
9. What open loop about *you* should be remembered that is not already in PROFILE or WORKING?

## After the deck

**Continuous mode** — adaptive gap questions when learning stalls. Same draft → approve → write loop.
