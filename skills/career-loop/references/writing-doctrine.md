# Writing doctrine (portable)

For resume + cover inside a career-loop packet. Not a vendor ATS playbook.

## Resume

- Two pages of substance: `## Skills` plus enough bullets and words that a 1-pager squeeze FAILs the gate.
- Quantified outcomes from the operator's own bank. Empty source → `could_not_verify`, do not invent.
- Show career length. Do not drop early roles to look "senior-only." Speak to gaps with a labeled sourced line. Do not invent filler jobs.

## Cover

- Open with a first-person lived hook. Do not open by explaining the company.
- `freeze/people-research.md` exists first. HM + recruiter: source or `could_not_verify`.
- Research changes **our** story. Do not write "I saw you volunteer" / "I know you have kids."
- No stock "I build the AI systems that sellers actually run daily" slogan.
- No em dashes (U+2014). Date en-dashes (U+2013) are OK.
- Letterhead: operator name + their LinkedIn URL.

## Send

Draft is not send. One role at a time. Human approve is bound to that company.
